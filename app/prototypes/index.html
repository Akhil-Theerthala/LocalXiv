My pick for long desktop reading: Focus

It gives the article the most uninterrupted space. The library grid appears when you need another paper, and the reading toolbar stays compact.

Choose Outline if jumping between sections matters more. Choose Study if you want to check an explanation against its source.

Try the details that change how reading feels

Open the library, change text size with Aa, switch themes, follow a citation, and try Focus mode. The bottom bar switches layouts while keeping the same paper.

Motion is limited to opening panels, changing layouts, and button feedback. The article stays still while you read.
