Settings

Conversion and reading work without AI. To generate overviews and discover related papers, connect an OpenAI-compatible provider.

Need a provider? Try Groq

For fast responses, try openai/gpt-oss-120b or qwen/qwen3.8-27b. Use https://api.groq.com/openai/v1 as the base URL. Their free tier currently allows 1,000 requests/day and 30/minute, with token quotas too. Qwen allows at most 16,384 response tokens; lower the AI request limits for your plan. Get a key · Check limits.

Keys are stored in macOS Keychain, never in this page.

Overviews send paper text to this provider. Recommendations send up to ten saved titles and a small set of public abstracts, once a day or after you add a paper.
