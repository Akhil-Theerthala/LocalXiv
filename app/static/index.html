AI connectionProvider, model, and API key

Connect an OpenAI-compatible provider for overviews and related papers. Conversion and reading work without AI.

Keys are stored in macOS Keychain, never in this page.

Requires an image-capable model. Reads local PNG/JPEG figures, including architectures and frameworks, in groups of up to six per request. Saves the combined text and figure understanding for both blogs and bentos, and checks generated diagrams.
What is sent to your provider?

Overviews send paper text and, when figure inspection is enabled, selected figure images. Recommendations send up to ten saved titles and a small set of public abstracts, once a day or after you add a paper.

Need a provider? Try Groq

For fast responses, try openai/gpt-oss-120b or qwen/qwen3.8-27b. Use https://api.groq.com/openai/v1 as the base URL. Their free tier currently allows 1,000 requests/day and 30/minute, with token quotas too. Qwen allows at most 16,384 response tokens; lower the AI request limits for your plan.
