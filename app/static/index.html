Welcome to LocalXiv

Your papers stay on this Mac. Each one has three views.

  • Overview: one figure that tells why the work was needed, how it works, and what it achieved.
  • Blog: an article that builds the context, with each claim linked to its passage.
  • Paper: the original text, set for reading.

The model ID from your provider’s model list.

Sets how long the model reasons before each Overview and Blog step. More effort takes longer and uses more tokens. In LocalXiv’s tests, more effort made Blogs more accurate and did not change Overviews, so Auto uses Medium for Blogs and Low for Overviews. Models without reasoning ignore this setting.

Overview and Blog

Tone and Blog length apply to Blogs. A Large Blog can be longer when the paper needs it. When AI is connected, imported papers are read in the background for later Blogs and Overviews. Papers imported without AI are read the first time you ask for an Overview or a Blog. Overviews and Blogs send paper text to your provider.
