# LocalXiv 0.1.0

Read arXiv papers comfortably on your Mac, then take them to Kindle. This release adds Chat, Highlights, and Notes to the reader, and takes the Notes out of the Paper: into the EPUB that goes to your Kindle, onto one Notes page for the whole library, and across the versions of a Paper.

## Changes

- **Chat and Ask.** Select words in a Paper and ask about them, or ask about the whole Paper. The answer cites the passages it used, and each citation opens its passage. Chat uses its own model and key, separate from the Overview and Blog model, and can run on a model on this Mac. A question keeps its follow-ups.
- **Highlights and Notes.** Select words and choose one of four colours: Key point, Question, Use in my work, or Disagree. Add a Note to a Highlight, or a Paper note on the whole Paper. The cards sit in the margin beside their words, and All Notes lists every Highlight, Note, and Question card of a Paper with search, four arrangements, and Note groups. The keyboard changes a colour in All Notes.
- **Notes survive a new version.** After a reimport, each Highlight's words are searched again in the new text. A Highlight whose words changed is marked as moved, and one whose words are gone stays as Not found, with its quote and its Note. The Markdown export and the JSON backup keep the Notes outside the library, and Restore Notes reads the backup back.
- **Notes in the EPUB.** Export EPUB with Notes, and the Include Highlights and Notes checkbox of Send to Kindle, mark the words of each Highlight in its colour inside the book, with a number that links to a Highlights and Notes chapter at the end. The chapter lists each Highlight with its section, quote, colour, Note, and questions with their answers, and each quote links back to its passage.
- **A Notes page.** A Notes row under Library opens every Highlight, Note, and Question card of the library, grouped by Paper, with search, four colour toggles, and a toggle for each `#tag` written in a Note. A row opens the Paper at the card.
- **Copy Notes to a new version.** When a version of a Paper opens with no Notes and another version in the library has some, the margin offers to copy them, and the Paper menu has Copy Notes from v<n>. Each copied Highlight is searched in the new version.
- **Links back into LocalXiv.** Each quote in the Markdown export carries a `localxiv://` link that opens the Paper at the passage. A bookmarklet in the README adds the arXiv or alphaXiv page you are on with one click.
- **More papers convert.** More TeX passes handle parser defects found in the audit, and the parser corpus has an allow list for the output changes they intend. The conversion and the Blog PDF export stay inside their sandboxes.
- **Cleanup.** The fixes of the architecture review, and the removal of the tutorial route, the usage table, the stored SVG copies, and the unused digests.

## Verification and limitations

The release workflow checks the source, builds the portable app, runs native renderer tests, and verifies the packaged runtime after moving the app. Attached assets include the verification report, runtime inventory, exact app source, dependency source inputs, and SHA-256 checksums.

For this release, ruff found no problems, and the component suite passed: 181 tests, 1 skipped (the live Paper search). The 25-paper parser corpus gate reports 0 papers with new problems against the previous main. The EPUB with Notes passes EPUBCheck, and the marks, the chapter, and the links were checked in the Mac's browser engine. The release notes in `docs/highlights-notes-chat-plan-2026-10-18.md` list each check of the three Notes releases.

Open items:

- No Kindle device read the EPUB with Notes. An e-ink Kindle may show the colour, the underline, both, or neither. The chapter and its links are standard EPUB.
- The Mac app was type-checked, not run, for this release. The sidebar row, the menu items, and the `localxiv://` links were checked in the browser shell.
- No VoiceOver pass and no Reduce Motion pass ran on the Notes page and the offer card.
- A formula inside a Highlight is not marked in the EPUB. The text around it is.
- The open items of 0.0.19 stay: 17 corpus papers end with only the original PDF, the local LaTeXML fallback converts no corpus paper, and the app bundles older Pandoc and EPUBCheck versions than the corpus.

Offline tests use scripted provider responses. They verify recovery and export behavior, not live-provider reliability, scientific accuracy, generation speed, or cost.

AI explanations and Chat remain experimental. Generation may take several minutes or fail, and generated claims need checking against the paper. AI requests send selected paper content, and for Chat the Highlights and Notes of the Paper, to your configured provider and may incur charges. Paper search sends the typed text to Semantic Scholar and arXiv. Importing, reading, highlighting, and exporting papers do not require AI credentials.

## Installation

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or newer. Download the DMG and drag LocalXiv into Applications. Finish active jobs before updating. Your library and settings remain on your Mac. Updater-enabled builds can use **LocalXiv → Check for Updates…** once the signed feed is published.

This preview is ad hoc signed, without Apple Developer ID signing or notarization. macOS may block the first launch. The Sparkle update feed is signed separately with the project's local update key. Separate-Mac installation and actual Kindle delivery are not covered by the automated checks.
