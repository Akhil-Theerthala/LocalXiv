# LocalXiv 0.0.14

Read arXiv papers comfortably on your Mac, then take them to Kindle. This preview draws every figure, Overview and Blog, through one figure library: the model authors content and structure, and the application lays out, renders, and checks the drawing.

## Changes

- One figure library, `papers/figures/`, replaces the model-drawn SVG path. A Blog figure is now one Scene panel laid out at the article width, with the same 14-unit text, palette, and checks as the Overview. The four-attempt SVG repair loop and the smolagents dependency are gone.
- The Blog runs on the Overview's coordinator. Selection, narrative, authoring, each figure, and review are direct structured requests with the rejected answer carried into the correction; one narrative revision is allowed per run.
- The drawing vocabulary the model reads is generated from the schema, so the card, the JSON schema, and the validator cannot drift. It gains arrows between side-by-side panels and a `chart` node for line and scatter data.
- The Overview density floor rises from 30 to 40 text runs per million square units.
- `tools/overview_run.py --kind blog` generates a Blog for one library paper and reports each figure's status and correction count.

## Verification and limitations

The release workflow checks the source, builds the portable app, runs native renderer tests, and verifies the packaged runtime after moving the app. Attached assets include the verification report, runtime inventory, exact app source, dependency source inputs, and SHA-256 checksums.

Local verification ran 158 tests with native rendering enabled, all passing, and a byte comparison of 31 saved scenes before and after the refactor. The live pilot in `docs/verification/2026-09-18-figure-library-pilot.md` ran three Overviews and two Blogs with deepseek-flash: every Overview passed its checks at densities 54 to 63; every planned Blog figure was drawn within one correction; one of two Blogs was delivered. The pilot found three defects, all fixed in this release. Its open items remain: a review finding on an accepted figure can spend the figure's remaining budget on a redraw that fails a limit by one character, and an article correction can collide with the word limit and fail the run.

Offline tests use scripted provider responses. They verify recovery and export behavior, not live-provider reliability, scientific accuracy, generation speed, or cost.

AI explanations remain experimental. Generation may take several minutes or fail, and generated claims need checking against the paper. AI requests send selected paper content and, where applicable, images to your configured provider and may incur charges. Importing, reading, and exporting papers do not require AI credentials.

## Installation

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or newer. Download the DMG and drag LocalXiv into Applications. Finish active jobs before updating. Your library and settings remain on your Mac. Updater-enabled builds can use **LocalXiv → Check for Updates…** once the signed feed is published.

This preview is ad hoc signed, without Apple Developer ID signing or notarization. macOS may block the first launch. The Sparkle update feed is signed separately with the project's local update key. Separate-Mac installation and actual Kindle delivery are not covered by the automated checks.
