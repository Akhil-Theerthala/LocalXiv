# UI polish audit, 2026-10-30

This audit looks at each step of LocalXiv in the Mac app itself, to find what keeps the app from feeling premium, polished, and smooth. It builds on [the UI polish audit of 2026-10-21](ui-polish-audit-2026-10-21.md), which read the code and browser screenshots but never ran the Mac app. This audit covers three things that audit did not:

- The Native shell as a reader sees it: the first frames at launch, the sidebar, the toolbar, the menus, the context menus, the keys, and the Settings window.
- The parts added since 2026-10-24: the Mermaid Figure renders with the hand-drawn look, the Component hover, Blog Tone, Chat in the Blog, Find Paper, and the Code link.
- The items that the 2026-10-21 audit left open or did not check.

[The premium Mac app research](premium-mac-research-2026-10-30.md) adds 54 principles that [the UI rule book](ui-rule-book-2026-10-21.md) does not state. The audit used both.

## Fix status

On 2026-10-30, 71 of the 74 items were fixed. Three items are not fixed:

- **VIS-2.** A layered app icon needs Xcode and Icon Composer. This Mac has only the Command Line Tools.
- **LAUNCH-1.** Its After is "Default: no change". DLG-1 fixes the hidden Save and Skip Setup buttons.
- **DLG-3.** The snapshot draws the web view with no background, so the colour difference needs a capture of the real Settings window first.

The user chose these answers for the group B items:

- **PAPER-2.** The column moves to the centre while the margin is empty. The width stays the same, so no line reflows.
- **PAPER-6.** Option A. The Margins choice sets the text width, and the margin cards fold to markers when they do not fit.
- **CHROME-1.** The native controls are disabled while a web dialog is open. The web scrim stays.
- **FIG-12.** As written.
- **DLG-4.** Native pop-up buttons and layout (b), grouped rows, in the Settings window only.

A review of the whole change and the app runs found 12 more defects, and all of them are fixed. Two examples: the Overview Contents left out steps whose label Pele moves to the titles layer, and it listed the steps in reverse order. A local `selectedRow` in `syncChrome()` hid the new `selectedRow()` function, and every page outside the reader showed an error.

These checks have not run yet:

- **KEY-1, KEY-2, KEY-4, KEY-5, and NOTE-4.** They need real keys, menus, and context menus, so they need a foreground run.
- **The Settings half of KEY-5.** One Tab stop with no visible focus most likely remains in the Settings window.
- **FIG-2 and FIG-6.** They show only in an Overview or a Blog that is made after the change.

## How the audit ran

1. Six journeys ran the Mac app with `tools/native_shot.py` on a copy of the library: three Papers, each with an Overview and a Blog. The installed app, its library, and its settings did not change, and no AI request was sent. The step files are in [`verification/2026-10-30-native-journeys/`](verification/2026-10-30-native-journeys/). The snapshots are not in the repository. The snapshot and dump names in this audit are the files that `python3 tools/native_shot.py --library LIBRARY --steps docs/verification/2026-10-30-native-journeys/jN.json --out OUT` writes into `OUT`. Journeys j1 to j5 took 112 snapshots and dumps of the native chrome, in Light, Sepia, and Dark, at 720, 900, 1180, and 1500 points wide, and with an empty library. Journey j6 ran in the foreground with the user's approval and tested the keys, the menus, and the context menus.
2. Five research lanes searched the web with Exa and kept 54 principles.
3. Eight auditors walked one journey or one cross-cutting concern each (launch and Home, the Library and the chrome, the Paper, the Overview and the Blog, Notes and Chat, dialogs and Settings, motion, and the visual system). One more auditor walked the foreground journey. Together they reported 147 findings.
4. Two skeptics with different lenses checked the findings of each auditor. One checked the evidence in the snapshots and the code. The other checked the recorded decisions, the 2026-10-21 fixes, and the Conflict index of the rule book. A finding that one skeptic refuted was dropped, except a high finding that the other skeptic confirmed. The skeptics kept 111 findings and refuted 36.
5. A merger joined the findings that name one defect into one item and checked that each finding is in exactly one item. Then a check of the snapshots added FIG-13. The result is 74 items.
6. The merger did not see the skeptics' notes. So a last pass applied the skeptics' corrected fixes to 39 items, and lowered the severity of 6 items.

The audit kept the recorded decisions: `scale(.97)` is the press feedback, the Reading theme colours the whole window, and the B items of the 2026-10-21 audit stay as they are.

### What the foreground run showed works

- ⌘N, ⇧⌘L, ⌘K, ⌘[, and ⌘] act at once, and the toolbar title names the new page.
- ⌘1, ⌘2, and ⌘3 change the view within 80 ms, with no cross-fade.
- ⌃⌘H paints a Highlight at once. ⌃⌘N opens the Note editor with the focus in its field.
- Esc closes an empty Note and keeps the focus on its card, with a ring.
- ⌥⌘T hides the toolbar with no jump of the text.
- The first Tab from the toolbar search moves to the Home field with a visible ring.
- **Paper › Mark as Read** updates the counts at once, and the item changes to **Mark as Unread**.
- The menu bar has the standard order, ellipses, Undo and Redo, a Find submenu, and Hide Sidebar and Hide Toolbar items that follow the state.

### What the audit could not check

- Real Liquid Glass, the label of the selected toolbar segment, and the colour of the sidebar selection. The snapshot draws the window with `cacheDisplay`, which cannot draw glass. `app/macos/Automation.swift:294-298` records this limit. In the snapshots, the selected segment has no label and the sidebar selection is black when the focus is outside the sidebar. These are not app defects. Look at the real window on macOS 26 and on macOS 27.
- Motion between two frames. A snapshot is a composite, so a frame taken 40 to 80 ms after a change cannot prove that no transition frame exists.
- macOS 26. All runs were on macOS 27.2.
- VoiceOver, Full Keyboard Access, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, and an accent colour other than blue.
- Chat with a real Chat model (streaming, Stop, and citations), because the audit sent no AI requests.
- The Reference preview in the Paper, Paper figures and display equations, Suggestions under the Home field, and the selection toolbar in the Blog. The scripted steps did not reach them.
- The time to first content at launch, Sepia on Home, the Library, Notes, and Settings, Settings in Dark, and the toolbar in full screen.

At the end of journey j5 the app did not quit within the time limit. j5-19 shows a save sheet from **Export › Highlights and Notes…**, which the script left open. The audit treats this as an effect of the script, not as an app defect.
