# UX audit: friction in launch, Overview, Blog, and the Paper reader

Status on 2026-09-25, end of day. Done: all launch items (launch now opens Home, setup opens once, and generation needs AI), Overview items 2 and 3, the Blog title, all Paper reader items, and all navigation items. Open by the user's decision: Overview item 1 (phone width) and Blog item 2 (citation links). The Blog heading uses a generated title, or the plan's question for older Blogs.

Audited on 2026-09-25 at `overview-equations` (`78d22bc`), with a fresh library (`--data-dir /tmp/localxiv-audit`) that holds only the Attention sample. Each finding names what a reader sees, the evidence, and the fix. Priority 1 blocks or misleads a reader. Priority 2 adds effort on every visit. Priority 3 is polish.

## Launch and setup

1. **Every launch opens the sample paper (priority 1).** The startup code in `app/static/app.js:826` opens `1706.03762v7` whenever it is in the library. A reader with twenty papers still lands on Attention, not on Home, the Library, or the paper they read last. Fix: open the last-read paper, and open the sample only on the first launch.
2. **Setup never opens by itself (priority 1).** `app.js:828` opens the setup dialog only when the library is empty. The server installs the sample on first start, so the library is never empty and the new reader never sees setup or the tour. Fix: gate setup on `onboarding_complete` alone.
3. **Generate without AI fails after the fact (priority 1).** With no provider, **Generate overview** queues a job. The job fails with "Overview failed · Choose a provider model first." and offers **Retry**, which fails again for the same reason. The POST handler in `app/server.py` does not check the AI setup before `app.submit`. Fix: when `settings.model` or `has_key` is missing, label the button **Connect AI to generate** and open setup. Reject the request in the server with the same message.
4. **Home has nothing for a returning reader (priority 2).** Home shows only the link field. Fix: show the last three papers the reader opened under the field.

## Overview

1. **The figure cannot be read on a phone (priority 1).** At 375 px the 1000-unit figure scales to about 350 px, so its 14-unit text draws at about 5 px. The Figure library already renders a panel at 640 units (`Figure(width=640).build(..., frame='panel')`). Fix: below 700 px, show each Overview panel as its own figure, in panel order, from the same Scene.
2. **The figure repeats what is around it (priority 2).** The figure sits in a card inside the page card. Its header repeats the paper title that is directly above it. A small caption under it repeats the figure's own footer. Fix: draw the figure without the outer card, drop the `LOCALXIV · <title>` line from the on-screen render, and drop the duplicate caption. Keep all three in the PNG and PDF exports, where the figure travels alone.
3. **Equations were raw text (fixed on `overview-equations`).** Scenes wrote `d_k`, `W^Q`, and `K^T`, and the figure showed the underscores and carets. The Figure library now typesets them. Saved Overviews keep their old render until the reader regenerates them.

## Blog

1. **The article has no way in and no map (priority 2).** The Blog starts with body text. It has no title, no one-line lead, no reading time, and no section list, although the Paper tab has **Paper sections**. Fix: show the plan's contribution sentence as the lead, the word count as minutes, and the `##` headings in the same side list the Paper tab uses.
2. **Citations are removed from view (priority 2).** `cleanOverviewCitations` strips every `[p00017]` marker, so a reader cannot check a claim against the paper. Fix: draw each citation as a small superscript link that opens the Paper at that passage, as a Component hover click does.
3. **Phone width reads well.** The serif body, line height, and sticky tab bar work at 375 px. Keep them.

## Paper reader

These findings are task 3. They need no work now, and they set the order for later work.

1. **Display equations look unfinished (priority 2).** In Section 3.2.1, `Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QK^T/√d_k)V` aligns left, has no equation number, and its parentheses do not grow around the tall fraction. Inline fractions such as `1/√d_k` draw very small and push the lines apart. The math is native MathML (58 `math` elements in `ch004.xhtml`). Fix in `reading.css`: center `math[display=block]` with its number at the right margin. Fix in conversion: set `stretchy="true"` on the fence `mo` elements around a fraction, and keep the source equation number.
2. **The Abstract starts with the arXiv license note (priority 2).** The first line of the sample Abstract is "Provided proper attribution is provided, Google hereby grants permission...". Fix: move the license paragraph out of the Abstract in conversion, to the end of the front matter.
3. **The section list and the floating Sections pill duplicate each other (priority 3).** At 1024 px the side list and the **Sections · Aa** pill both show. Fix: show the pill only when the side list is hidden.

## Navigation and settings

1. **The Library button stays selected inside a paper (priority 3).** After the reader opens a paper from the Library, **Library** keeps its selected style. Fix: clear it in `openPaper`.
2. **The Library list shows no state (priority 2).** A row shows title, authors, and ID. It does not say whether an Overview or a Blog exists, when the paper was added, or which paper the reader opened last. The count sits small and apart from the heading. Fix: add **Overview** and **Blog** marks and the added date to each row, and put the count next to the heading.
3. **"Language" chooses a tone (priority 3).** The Settings select labelled **Language** offers **Casual**. Fix: rename the label to **Tone**.
