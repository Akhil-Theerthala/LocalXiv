# Parser robustness and efficiency

Implementation and the frozen-corpus evaluation are complete. The final parser produced validated EPUBs for **49 of 76 sampled documents**. The other 27 retain their original PDFs and failure reports. These numbers describe conversion coverage, not a guarantee that every source detail or device rendering is flawless.

## Sample and results

The sample contains 36 papers from 12 conference groups, 28 papers from seven non-CS subject groups, and 12 notes, lectures, or tutorials. Exact arXiv versions, sampling frames, seed, provenance, and the development/holdout assignment are retained in `robustness-sample.json`. No failed paper was replaced. Conference membership comes from author-reported metadata; two Findings entries are labeled companion tracks. Non-CS selection uses seeded monthly listing clusters after API failures. This is not a uniform sample of all arXiv or every leading conference.

Final coverage is 28/36 conference selections, 17/28 non-CS subject selections, and 4/12 notes or lectures. Notes remain the weakest group. The historical set is a repair set and is not an independent estimate of general coverage.

The first holdout result remains unchanged in `holdout-01`. Later affiliation and bibliography repairs used holdout findings, so their reruns are explicitly post-evaluation. Revision 03 evaluated every sampled and historical input. Revision 04 added two narrow source normalizations. Scanning all 93 original inputs and retained normalized source trees found affected syntax in only two papers; both were rerun in `candidate-04`. The other revision-03 outputs are reused under the scope record in `robustness-final-increment.json`. This is incremental validation, not a claim that every output was generated by one identical binary.

The final development count is one lower than the first candidate because `2502.02693v1` was previously a misleading success. Its hosted HTML drops physical-unit denominators. The parser now rejects that route; its source route also lacks a supported diagram conversion. The original PDF is retained instead of accepting incorrect units.

## What changed

The application tries Pandoc, exact-version arXiv HTML, LaTeXML, then the original PDF. The HTML route verifies the paper version, caches article and figure hashes, restricts resource retrieval, and packages locally inside the isolated worker. Unsupported hosted content and unavailable resources fail explicitly. Conversion and reading do not require AI credentials.

Source repairs address package-defined math, literal template abbreviations, author affiliations and contact details, bibliography identifiers and RevTeX wrappers, algorithm dialects, boxed prose, grouped tables, shared figure captions, print-only table controls, and checkmark glyphs. Scientific-notation normalization retains numeric values and units. A conservative HTML check rejects slash-form siunitx units in retained source because the hosted renderer was observed to truncate their denominators.

The completeness work includes ordered prose and structure snapshots, source/PDF anchors, table-cell and span checks, figure/resource/link checks, MathML structure, equation-image validation, and deliberately corrupted outputs that prove the comparison checks can detect lost or altered content. Every successful conversion passes EPUBCheck for both EPUB profiles. EPUBCheck alone is not the content criterion.

## Completeness limits

All 49 final EPUB outputs have content audits. **28 have no flags in the implemented checks; 21 retain heuristic review flags.** These flags are not 21 confirmed broken EPUBs. Every remaining caption and note anchor was located after accounting for word boundaries, accents, or mathematical subscripts. Targeted prose review also explained inserted heading numbers and changes in PDF reading order. The original audit flags are preserved rather than silently rewritten as passes.

Targeted review explains all flagged discrepancies for another 12 papers, giving **40/76 inputs with either no automated flags or explained flags in the checks performed**. Nine converted papers retain unresolved heuristic findings. This is a bounded check result, not a claim of flawless output. Exact contexts and carried-forward identical-content evidence are in `.verification/robustness/final-review-adjudications.json`.

Some prose-anchor and graphics-count discrepancies remain unadjudicated. The checks and selected rendered reviews do not prove every character, mathematical expression, figure panel, or reading-device layout is faithful. Wide tables can exceed the preview viewport. A physical Kindle or document-delivery workflow was not tested. The per-paper report keeps conversion status, audit status, retained input payload, and retained PDF separate.

The 27 EPUB failures include unavailable TeX source, unsupported source syntax or diagrams, hosted-content errors, broken resources, and unsupported mathematical markup. The evaluation deliberately does not count PDF fallback as EPUB success. It retains the evidence needed to reproduce each failure.

## Measured efficiency

Equation rendering now batches a paper's formulas and reuses identical formula renderings within that paper. Three alternating trials used identical ordered chapters with no concurrent conversions. Every rewritten chapter and generated PNG matched exactly.

| Equation-rendering input | Earlier median | Batched median | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| UniAD, 2212.10156v2 | 2.790 s | 2.277 s | 18.4% less time |
| Physics, 2006.01831v2 | 15.860 s | 15.904 s | Effectively unchanged |

These measurements exclude retrieval, source parsing, and EPUB validation. They do not establish a universal speedup.

One controlled recovery-order comparison on identical cached inputs improved from **206.582 s to 24.889 s**, an **88% reduction**. The old order spent about 182 seconds on a failing LaTeXML attempt before using HTML. The new order reached the same HTML output directly after Pandoc failed. The content comparison found no differences. This is one measured failure path, not a population-wide timing claim.

LaTeXML also stops after an error that would already disqualify its result, rather than consuming the rest of its timeout. Logs remain available and the isolated process group is cleaned up. Controlled evidence is retained in `benchmarks/equation-batch-02` and `benchmarks/recovery-order-01`.

## Tests and repeatability

The final change passes **193 existing regression tests and 51 focused robustness tests**. Syntax compilation and `git diff --check` also pass. Real conversions, source/PDF audits, rendered reviews, and exact table/bibliography/affiliation witnesses are separate evidence beyond those tests.
